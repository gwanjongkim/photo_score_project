# Dataset protocols

| Dataset | Labels and scale | Normative split/protocol | Evaluation | Evidence/status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AVA | ~250k images; histogram of ratings 1–10, ~200 ratings/image; MOS is histogram expectation | NIMA: random 80/20 paper split. RGNet/A-LAMP/AADB-paper comparisons: literature ~230k train/~20k test. These are not proven identical filename partitions. | Distribution EMD; MOS LCC/PLCC and SRCC; binary accuracy at threshold 5 where specified | S01 Sec. III; S03 Sec. 5.1; S04 Sec. 4.1; VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE, HIGH |
| AADB | 10k Flickr images; 5 ratings/image, mean score; 11 attributes; rater IDs; paper rescales target to `[0,1]` | 8,500 train, 500 validation, 1,000 test. Pair training should preserve within-rater sampling evidence. | Test SRCC; optional AVA threshold accuracy; cross-dataset matrix | S03 Secs. 3,5.1–5.2; S04 Sec. 4.1; VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE, HIGH |
| ICAA17K | ~17k images; 30 color combinations/types, >50 scenes; color-aesthetic rating, confidence `[0,1]`; auxiliary holistic score in joint setting | Exact filename split and numeric label scale not sufficiently visible in paper text; obtain from official dataset manifest and record its version/hash | SRCC and LCC/PLCC; do not substitute generic AVA aesthetic MOS | S06 Secs. 4–5; S07 official repository; VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE, MEDIUM |
| KonIQ-10k | 10,073 1024×768 images; five-point ACR counts, MOS, SD, user-z-scored MOS | For MUSIQ: random 80/20 per run, 10 runs. For KonCept512: official published partition; do not conflate these. | SRCC/SROCC and PLCC; cross-dataset LIVE-in-the-Wild separately | S08 Sec. 4.1; S10; S11 score schema; VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE/DATASET_DOCUMENTATION, HIGH |
| FLIVE / PaQ-2-PiQ | ~40k full Flickr pictures and ~120k rated patches; MOS is perceptual technical quality, not aesthetics | Use official train/validation/test manifests. Full-size test has at least one dimension >640. Image and patch partitions/tasks must remain distinguishable. | Full-image and patch SRCC/PLCC; no unreported calibration | S12; S08 Sec. 4.3/Table 1; VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE, HIGH |
| SPAQ | 11,125 smartphone photos; MOS plus attributes (paper-specific subset usage varies) | Use official split/literature protocol named by the method. MDIQA’s five technical heads use its own option file; ICAA benchmark may use colorfulness only. | SRCC/LCC/PLCC according to source | S06 Table 4; S08 Sec. 4.1; VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE, MEDIUM |
| TID2013/LIVE | Synthetically distorted/reference IQA datasets with their own MOS/DMOS conventions | NIMA paper uses 80/20 and cross-dataset tests. Content leakage requires reference-content-separated splitting where dataset protocol demands it. | LCC/SRCC and distribution metrics where NIMA constructs distributions | S01 Sec. III/Tables II–IV; VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE, MEDIUM |

Correlation protocol: SRCC is computed directly on paired image predictions and ground truth ranks. PLCC is direct Pearson unless a paper explicitly states a nonlinear logistic mapping. None of the opened NIMA, RGNet, AADB, MUSIQ, or A-LAMP passages authorize silently fitting a logistic curve. If later official code performs fitting, report both raw and fitted values and cite the code path.
