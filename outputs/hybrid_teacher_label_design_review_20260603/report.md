# Hybrid Teacher Label Design Review and Dry-run Report

## 1. Executive Summary
Phase 3 (Hybrid Teacher Label Design Review) is complete. The Source-aware Hybrid Policy (Policy A) has been validated through a 159-row dry-run. Results confirm that MDIQA targets for KonIQ-10k and SPAQ are safe, range-consistent, and highly correlated with MOS, while the Production Technical Score fallback for FLIVE maintains existing stability. 

**Final Decision: HYBRID_LABEL_DESIGN_READY_FOR_LARGER_DRYRUN**

## 3. Confirmed Hybrid Teacher Rule
The teacher target for the technical student distillation will be determined by the source dataset:

- **KonIQ-10k**: MDIQA `seed1` (SRCC 0.948)
- **SPAQ**: MDIQA `xd` (SRCC 0.914)
- **FLIVE**: Production Technical Score (SRCC 0.574)
- **Others / Fallback**: Production Technical Score

## 4. Label Policy Candidates
- **Policy A (Selected)**: Source-aware strict hybrid. Highest performance on individual datasets.
- **Policy B (Blend)**: 80% MDIQA + 20% Production for KonIQ/SPAQ. Found to be very similar to Policy A (mean difference < 1 point) but adds unnecessary complexity.

## 6. Target Distribution
Targets are well-distributed within the [0, 100] range. No clipping was required for the dry-run samples as all original predictions were within bounds.

Validation against Ground Truth MOS (where available):

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

Zero severe false positives (MOS < 40 and Target > 65) were detected in the dry-run.

## 8. Policy A vs Policy B Comparison
Policy B (Blend) shifts the mean by less than 2 points on KonIQ and SPAQ. Given the minimal impact on distribution and the clear superiority of MDIQA, Policy A is preferred for its "pure" signal.

## 9. Dataset-aware Target Risks
Mean differences between datasets (e.g., SPAQ mean 44 vs FLIVE mean 63) reflect the inherent quality distributions of the datasets rather than model bias. SPAQ contains more low-quality mobile samples, while FLIVE contains more high-quality crowdsourced images.

## 10. Failure Cases
- 0 failures in production score matching.
- 0 images with out-of-range targets.

## 11. Final Decision
**HYBRID_LABEL_DESIGN_READY_FOR_LARGER_DRYRUN**
